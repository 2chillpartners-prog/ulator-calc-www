Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Overview

Tool Belt Logic ("the App," "we," "us") is a multi-mode professional construction calculator with voice interaction, AI-powered assistance, and construction plan/PDF tools. It is available on iOS, Android, and the web (at tb-logic.com), and is operated by Chill Inc. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who processes it, and your choices.

You can use the calculator without creating an account. The plan reader requires a free Tool Belt Logic account, because your plans and the work you do on them are saved to that account. Other features — syncing your work across devices, the web app, working together in crews, and the AI assistant — also use an account and/or send content to the processors listed below. Not every feature is available to every user; features you cannot access do not collect data from you.

Accounts and Sign-In

A Tool Belt Logic account is one email address. You sign in with a 6-digit code we email to that address (and then a password you choose), or with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Your account owns your subscription and your saved work, and lets you sign in on more than one device (phone, tablet, and the web).

When you create an account we store, on our server: your email address, the name you enter, a phone number if you choose to add one, your password if you set one (stored only as a one-way hash, never readable), account created and last sign-in timestamps, and the identifiers of the devices you sign in on. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we store the identifier those services return, and the name and email they share, so we can recognize you next time. Signed-in sessions are stored so you stay logged in. The calculator remains usable without an account.

What TB Logic Keeps, and Why

TB Logic keeps what it needs to work for you: to save your work, keep your devices in step, fix problems fast, and make its intelligence stronger for you.

Collected Automatically

  • Device Identifier: a random UUID generated on your device, used to keep your data separate and, once you sign in, to link your work to your account. On its own it is not your name, phone number, or Apple/Google account.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Reports (always on): when the App crashes, freezes, runs out of memory, or is shut down by your phone, it sends us a crash report so we can fix it: the error and where in the App it happened, your device model, operating system and App version, the screen you were on and the last few steps you took in the App, memory and app-state details, and a random device identifier. When you are signed in, the report is linked to your account (your account ID and email), so we can tell whether a crash keeps hitting the same person. New crashes are sent to us by email and push notification. Crash reports are kept for up to 12 months and are used only to find and fix problems. If the plan reader detects a display failure (for example, a frozen or blank screen), it may automatically send us a diagnostic report that can include reduced-size snapshots of what was on screen in the seconds before the failure — which may show part of a plan you had open — together with technical state about what the App was doing. These reports are used only to diagnose and fix the problem.
  • Usage Signals (when enabled): where you have agreed to AI data sharing, the App may send anonymous health and usage events — device identifier, App version, update id, and platform, plus which formulas or screens were used — so we can see what is working and what is breaking. These signals do not include your plans or the contents of your chats.

Sent When You Report a Problem ("Flag")

  • When you tap the flag button to report an issue, we receive your note, a screenshot of your screen at that moment (which may show part of a plan), any markup or photos you choose to attach, a short trace of what you did on that screen, and technical state about what the App was doing. This is sent so we can see exactly what you saw and fix it.

Provided by You (when you use AI, voice, or plan features)

  • Text you type or dictate in AI chat.
  • Voice recordings captured when you use voice input, used to produce a transcription and, for some replies, spoken audio.
  • Photos and images you submit for analysis or attach to a report.
  • PDF and plan files you upload, and the text and page images we extract from them.
  • Your selected calculator mode (e.g., "Construction"), for context.
  • When you give a voice command the App cannot handle on the device, a snapshot of the current context — the open formula's name and field values, and the open plan's name and sheet — so the assistant can act on it.

Your Saved Work

  • Your chats, uploaded plans and extracted text, jobs and folders, marks and takeoff lines, and (when you use them) schedule items are stored on our server and, when you are signed in, synced across your devices so you can pick up where you left off. Calculation history, preferences, and custom trade terms are kept on your device.

Calendar and Reminders

  • If you connect a calendar, the App can show your schedule inside Tool Belt Logic and add the events you create (by voice or by hand) to the calendar you choose — your phone's primary calendar or another one, whether that is Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or any calendar your phone syncs. To display your schedule, the App reads your calendar events on your device; it does not change or delete your existing events, and it does not use your calendar for anything other than showing it to you and adding the entries you ask for. Your schedule items are also saved to your account so they sync across your devices.

Working Together — Crews and Shared Jobs

Where the crew feature is available, you can invite people to a crew (by an email link or your phone's share sheet) and share a job with them. When you do, the people you invite can see the shared job's contents.

  • Crew members see the job's plan files, each member's marks and counts on those plans, the job's message thread, and messages pinned to a spot on a sheet.
  • A member's job title (foreman, laborer, or whatever is typed) is visible to the crew. By virtue of an invite, a member's email is visible to the job's creator, and the creator's to the member.
  • Push notifications carry job activity to members' phones (through the Expo push service, using a device push token we store for that purpose).
  • Messages are stored once in the language they were written in, and translated on each reader's device for display.

Content you place in a shared job is visible to that job's crew. Content that is not shared to a job stays private to you.

How AI, Voice, and Plan Features Work (Third-Party Processing)

Before you use any AI feature for the first time, the App asks for your explicit consent. No data is sent for AI processing until you agree, and you can turn it off in Settings. When you use these features, we send the relevant content to the providers below, who process it under business/enterprise terms and do not use it to train their own models:

1. Anthropic — Claude (anthropic.com)

Receives your chat text, your selected mode, images or plan pages you ask us to analyze (we render plan pages to images for visual analysis), and — on a voice command the App cannot handle locally — the small on-screen snapshot described above. Used to generate AI answers and to analyze plans and images. Anthropic may keep request content for up to 30 days for safety and abuse monitoring, then deletes it. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

2. Deepgram (deepgram.com)

Receives your voice audio to convert speech to text, and receives answer text to produce spoken audio (text-to-speech), processed in real time and not retained after processing — every request carries Deepgram's opt-out from its model-improvement program. Voice is sent to Deepgram when our own speech model (above) is not used. See Deepgram's Privacy Policy.

3. RevenueCat (revenuecat.com)

Receives a random identifier for your app install and, once you sign in, your account ID, along with your purchase and subscription status, to manage subscriptions and verify purchases. When you delete your account, we delete your RevenueCat customer record. If you subscribe on the web, checkout is run by RevenueCat Web Billing using Stripe (stripe.com), which receives your email address and payment details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Stripe's Privacy Policy.

4. Resend (resend.com)

Sends transactional email on our behalf — your sign-in codes and crew invitations — and to do so receives the recipient email address. It also delivers crash and problem reports to us, which can include your account email. Resend keeps sent email for up to 30 days. See Resend's Privacy Policy.

5. Expo push service (expo.dev)

Delivers push notifications for crew and job activity to your device, using a device push token. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

6. Railway (railway.com)

Hosts our servers and the database that holds the data described in this policy. See Railway's Privacy Policy.

7. Cloud object storage

When shared jobs are configured to use it, plan files for a shared job are stored with a cloud object-storage provider (Amazon S3 or compatible) so crew members can download them. Where it is not configured, files are handed directly between devices.

PDF and plan text extraction is performed on our own servers using standard tools — we do not send your uploaded documents to a third-party document-parsing service. We also run our own AI models on rented cloud computers (RunPod), among them TB Brain, Tool Belt Logic's own AI — one of the two AIs we use, with Anthropic's. They can receive plan pages you work on with the AI (TB Brain reads plans too), photos you send to the AI chat (to read them before the answer is written), voice you record (to turn it into text), and the questions you ask the AI chat (TB Brain works on them too). Nothing that identifies you — no name, email, or account — is sent with that content, RunPod discards the results within minutes, and these models are ours: nobody else uses the content. How we use your content to improve our own tools is described under “Data Ownership & Improving the App” below.

Plan translation

If you work in a language other than the one a plan is drawn in, the App can translate the plan's labels and notes. To do this, the text written on the sheet (with the sheet number for context) is sent through our server to Anthropic for translation, and the result is stored on your device so the plan never needs translating twice. Numbers, dimensions, and sizes are never sent for translation and are never altered — they are locked before translation begins. Translations are marked in the App as machine translation and are subject to the accuracy disclaimer below.

Reading scanned pages — on your device only

Some plan pages are scans with no readable text. To name those sheets and make their references tappable, the App reads the lettering using your phone's own built-in text recognition (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on-device on Android). This happens entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded, and it works with no signal.

Cookies (Web App)

The web app sets a single session cookie (tb_session) when you sign in. It is httpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and exists only to keep you logged in. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use it for third-party advertising, and we set no advertising cookies.
  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not change or delete your existing calendar events, and we do not use your calendar for anything other than showing it to you inside the App and adding the entries you ask for.
  • Our third-party AI providers do not use your data to train their models.

How We Use Your Data

  • Your account: your email is used to sign you in, to sync your work across your devices, to send crew invitations you initiate, and to contact you about your account or support.
  • AI responses and plan analysis: content is sent to Anthropic (Claude) as described above to generate answers and analyze plans and images.
  • Voice: audio is sent to Deepgram to convert speech to text and to read answers aloud. We do not retain your voice recordings after processing; the resulting text may be saved as part of your chat.
  • Conversation and document history: your chats, jobs, and uploaded plans/extracted text are stored on our server, linked to your account (or device before you sign in), so you can return to them.
  • Crews: content you share to a job is made available to that job's crew members, and job activity is sent to them as push notifications.
  • Personalization: facts you share in conversation (e.g., preferred units, local codes) may be stored to personalize future answers.
  • Subscription management: your account ID (or, before you sign in, a random install identifier) is used with RevenueCat to verify subscription status.

Your Work Makes TB Logic Smarter, for You

You own your content. The way you work (your chats, your calculations, the corrections you make, the plans you run) is what teaches TB Logic to help you better. It's kept in our own secure database and used, including in de-identified or combined form, to make the app's tools and intelligence stronger for you and every builder who uses it.

  • Your data is never sold to any third party.
  • Third-party AI providers (Anthropic, Deepgram) do not use your data to train their models — every call uses business/enterprise terms.
  • Your data is not shared with other users except the content you choose to share into a crew job, which is visible to that job's crew.
  • You can delete your stored data, and your account, at any time (see below).

Data Storage and Security

  • Server-side data (accounts, conversations, jobs, uploaded documents, AI learning) is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Railway; shared-job plan files may be held in cloud object storage as described above.
  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS and isolated per account — no user can access another user's data except content shared into a crew job.
  • We use reasonable, industry-standard measures designed to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your chats, jobs, and uploaded documents are retained on our servers until you delete them — individually, or all at once — or until you delete your account. Local data (preferences, history, trade terms) is cleared by uninstalling the App or clearing app data. We may retain de-identified and/or aggregated data to improve the App.

Shared jobs: only a job's creator can delete a job for everyone; when they do, the job's content (including crew members' marks, counts, and uploads made on that job) is retained by the creator's account before the job is removed from the crew. Any member can leave a shared job at any time, which removes it from their devices; content they contributed stays with the job. Deleting a file removes its bytes from your account and devices, while the marks and takeoff lines recorded on it are kept as part of the job's record.

Deleting your account: you can delete your account from within the App (Settings → Account → Delete account), on our delete-account page, or by emailing support@toolbeltlogic.com. Deleting your account erases from our servers: your email and sign-in details, every signed-in device, your synced plans and their files, marks, measurements and takeoffs, saved calculations, schedule items, your chats and uploaded documents, and our AI records of your conversations. Jobs you created that nobody else is on are erased. If a crew is still working in a job you created, the job stays with them without a creator; what you added to any shared job stays with that job with your name removed, and your messages in it are deleted. Crash reports are kept (up to 12 months) with your account details removed. Deleting your account does not remove the copy on your own devices (uninstalling the App does), and it does not cancel a subscription — cancel that with Apple or Google.

When you delete your account, we delete it and everything tied to you: your plans, jobs, chats, settings, and the AI’s notes about you. One thing can stay: general building know-how the AI learned while working with you, such as which schedule to check before counting hold-downs. Before we keep any of it, we remove everything that could point back to you or your jobs: names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, job and plan names, prices, and measurements from your plans. Anything we can’t fully clean is deleted, and nothing we keep is used for anyone else until a person has reviewed it.

Deleting your data but keeping your account: email support@toolbeltlogic.com with “Data Deletion Request” in the subject, and we will erase everything your account keeps on our servers while keeping your sign-in and subscription (see our data-deletion page).
