Delete Your Data

Tool Belt Logic by Chill Inc.

You are in full control of your data. Tool Belt Logic stores most data locally on your device, and you can delete it at any time from within the app.

How to Delete Your Data

1. Delete Calculation History
Open the app → On the calculator, tap the clock button at the top left to open History → Tap the check-mark button at the top right → Tap the double check mark to pick everything → Tap "Delete". Do the same on the Formulas tab.
2. Delete Saved Items & Folders
Open the app → On the calculator, tap the bookmark button at the top left to open Saved → Tap the trash can at the top right → Tap "Clear All" to remove all saved measurements, formulas, and folders.
3. Delete Chat Conversations
Open the app → Open History (the clock button on the calculator) → Tap the Chat tab → Tap the check-mark button, pick the conversations, and tap "Delete".
4. Delete All Local Data
Uninstalling the app from your device will remove all locally stored data including history, saved items, preferences, and custom terms.

Types of Data Stored

On Your Device (Local)
Calculation history, saved measurements, folders, plans you opened, preferences, custom trade terms, and a random device ID. Without an account this stays on your device unless you choose to share it.
On Our Server
Chat conversations, shared links, documents uploaded for AI analysis, crash reports, and AI-learned knowledge. With a Tool Belt Logic account, also: your email address, name (and phone, if you add one), your synced plans and their files, marks, measurements, takeoffs, saved calculations and schedule. Account data is linked to your account; data from before you signed in is linked to your device ID.

Have an account? Delete it yourself

You can delete your account and everything it keeps on our servers right now, without waiting on us (in the app: Settings → Account & plan → tap your account → Delete my account):

Delete my account →

When you delete your account, we delete it and everything tied to you: your plans, jobs, chats, settings, and the AI’s notes about you. One thing can stay: general building know-how the AI learned while working with you, such as which schedule to check before counting hold-downs. Before we keep any of it, we remove everything that could point back to you or your jobs: names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, job and plan names, prices, and measurements from your plans. Anything we can’t fully clean is deleted, and nothing we keep is used for anyone else until a person has reviewed it.

Request Server Data Deletion

To request deletion of all server-side data associated with your device, please contact us at:

support@toolbeltlogic.com

Include "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm once complete.

Without an account, Tool Belt Logic does not collect your name, email address, or phone number, and your data is linked only to an anonymous device identifier. With an account, your data is linked to that account — see our Privacy Policy.
Data Retention
Local data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. Server-side data is kept until you delete it, delete your account, or ask us to delete it. Crash reports are kept for up to 12 months.
